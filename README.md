# 🚀 InterviewOS – AI Career Operating System

> **An intelligent interview preparation platform that learns from every interview and continuously evolves with the candidate.**

Unlike traditional interview practice tools, InterviewOS creates a personalized AI Career Twin that tracks progress, identifies weaknesses, predicts company readiness, and generates adaptive learning paths for top tech companies.

---

## ✨ Overview

InterviewOS is a full-stack AI-powered career preparation platform designed to help students and professionals prepare for software engineering interviews.

The platform combines AI-powered mock interviews, resume analysis, company-specific interview simulations, career analytics, and personalized roadmaps into one unified dashboard.

Instead of treating every interview independently, InterviewOS continuously learns from the user's performance and improves future interview sessions accordingly.

- Coding Interviews

- Text Interviews

## 📄 Resume Intelligence

Upload your resume and InterviewOS automatically extracts:

- Skills
- Projects
- Experience
- Education
- Technologies

The AI then asks interview questions directly from your resume to simulate real recruiter behavior.

---

## 🧠 Career Digital Twin

One of the most unique features of InterviewOS.

The system continuously builds a digital profile of the candidate by learning from:

- Resume
- Previous interviews
- Coding performance
- Communication skills
- Behavioral responses
- Career goals

Every future recommendation becomes personalized.

## 📈 Company Readiness Score

InterviewOS estimates interview readiness for multiple companies.

Example:

Google — 76%

Microsoft — 84%

Adobe — 91%

Atlassian — 87%

The score is calculated using weighted performance metrics collected throughout the platform.
